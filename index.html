FAQ
Common questions.
How does Axion generate signals?+

Axion runs four independent trading strategies modeled after renowned traders' methodologies. It analyzes real-time BTC price data across multiple timeframes, computes technical indicators (EMA, RSI, VWAP, Bollinger Bands, ATR), detects institutional structures (order blocks, fair value gaps), and generates signals with precise entry, stop loss, and take profit levels.

Is my money safe?+

We never hold your funds. Your capital stays on your exchange (Coinbase, Kraken, or Binance) at all times. You connect via API keys with trade-only permissions — we cannot withdraw funds. You can revoke access at any time.

What exchanges are supported?+

We support Coinbase, Kraken, and Binance — the three largest regulated exchanges. More exchanges including Bybit and KuCoin are on the roadmap.

Can I use it without auto-trading?+

Absolutely. Many users receive signals and execute manually. Each signal comes with entry, SL, and TP targets. Auto-execution is optional on Pro plans.

What's the minimum account size?+

No minimum, but we recommend $500+. Default risk is 1% per trade, so $500 risks $5 per signal.

Can I cancel anytime?+

Monthly and annual plans can be cancelled anytime — you keep access until the end of your billing period. Lifetime access is permanent and non-refundable.
